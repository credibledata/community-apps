Results vs. the simulation

The count as it comes in, against the forecast the simulation made before the polls closed, its other scenarios, and the swing in past midterms.

A partial count is not a result. Places count mail, early and election-day ballots in different orders, so early margins can move by several points. Comparisons below use only races with at least 90% of the expected vote counted.

Vote counts and race calls are from the Associated Press, as published in The New York Times’ results pages; the count is unofficial until certified. Past House popular votes are from Wikipedia (from the Clerk of the House; CC BY-SA 4.0) and presidential margins from the MIT Election Data and Science Lab. The forecast is the simulation’s own, frozen before polls closed. This project is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any of these sources.
